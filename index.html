About me

I'm a developer with 5 years of experience turning complex problems into clean, user-centric solutions. I care deeply about performance, accessibility, and the invisible polish that makes good products feel great.

My work lives at the intersection of engineering rigour and design sensibility — I believe the best interfaces are the ones that feel effortless, even when the underlying complexity is anything but.

When I'm not shipping code I'm probably reading design history, hiking, or reverse-engineering a UI I admire.
